# Surface brief: style explorations (styles/)

Scope: three homepage style explorations for Enero Marso Cafe, requested by the owner while the Canva design is pending. Mode: Persuade. Static HTML prototypes; the chosen direction is rebuilt in Next.js. Audience, job, constraints: see PRODUCT.md. Seed key b4c30a73 (assigned index 6). Owner pinned the deliverable: build three, not choose one before building.

## Direction contract

### A. Roast Label (assigned, candidate 6)
THESIS: the site is the cafe's coffee bag label: roast date, origin, notes, batch printed as proof of quality. Refuses the dark-brown latte-photo hero.
OWN-WORLD: saturated kraft ground, exactly two inks (label black, coffee-cherry red), no grays. Condensed poster caps, mono only for batch data. Stamps, perforations, die-cut label panels.
STORY: visitor reads today's roast as a label, trusts the coffee, picks a menu item, finds hours, orders.
FIRST VIEWPORT: one die-cut label filling the screen: logo stamp, today's roast data rows, menu-and-order actions printed as tear-off tabs.
FORM: coffee packaging label, list position 6, seed b4c30a73.
RAISES: from coiled earth tower: content set in strict horizontal courses, each row's measure fixed. From op-art textile: total two-ink commitment, zero gray. From drum machine row: one unbroken freshness line (roast to cup) runs the page as its spine.

### B. Premier Deco (Impeccable's pick, candidate 1)
THESIS: a 1930s Manila movie palace on premiere night: the menu is the letterboard, hours are showtimes. Refuses generic black-and-gold luxury.
OWN-WORLD: theater black, logo gold, deep oxblood velvet; stepped deco ornament, sunburst fans, marquee bulbs.
STORY: the visitor feels the occasion, reads "now serving", checks showtimes, reserves or orders.
FIRST VIEWPORT: a marquee with bulb-lit border holding the logo; letterboard menu and showtimes directly below; the actions are ticket stubs.
FORM: Manila Art Deco cinema, list position 1.

### C. Cherry Drench (challenger: seedbed lobes, competitive)
THESIS: one coffee-cherry red owns every screen; the interface floats over it as cream capsules built for fast ordering on phones. Refuses the café-as-living-room cliché.
OWN-WORLD: drenched cherry red, cream capsules and slabs, gold logo, ink #2b1d18, flat vector cast of cups, cherries, beans.
STORY: student or office worker lands, picks delivery/pickup/dine-in at once, scans favorites, goes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: floating capsule nav; huge flush-left headline; order-mode capsule switcher as the working action; vector cast spilling lower right.
FORM: seedbed lobes fused, challenger.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
